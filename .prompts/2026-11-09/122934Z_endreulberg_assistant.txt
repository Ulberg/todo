**What you can check now:**
1. **Policy:** PIM → **Microsoft Entra roles** → **Settings** → **Global Administrator**. It should show an activation maximum of 1 hour, MFA and justification required, and permanent eligibility allowed.
2. **The activation flow itself:** activate **platform-admin** under **My roles** → **Groups**. You're eligible for it and don't hold it, so this is a real activation with the same MFA and justification steps, capped at 1 hour.

1. **Run 1:** remove only your admin account's standing Global Admin. You then activate Global Admin through PIM, which is the real test. If it fails, the owner account and both break-glass accounts still have Global Admin.
