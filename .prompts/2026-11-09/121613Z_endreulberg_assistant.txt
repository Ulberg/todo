Not quite. Today's plan has no prod and no release step for the app lead. Here is what PLAN.md §2 currently describes:

1. **Who may merge app code:** an app developer opens a PR in `todo`, the app's repo lead (code owner) approves it, and it merges to `main`.
2. **Build:** app CI builds and signs the images and pushes them to ACR.
3. **Request:** the pr-bot opens a PR on `platform` with the new image tags and the current `platform.yaml`.
   - A change to image tags only that passes policy merges automatically.
   - Any `platform.yaml` change needs a platform lead's approval.
4. **Deploy:** `pulumi up` runs in the `dev` environment, which needs a platform lead's approval.

So there are two differences from your picture:
- **There's only one environment** (D7). `prod` is listed under "later" as a new Pulumi stack on the same code, and there's no release or promotion step.

- Platform leads would still own `platform.yaml` changes and anything that isn't a tag.
