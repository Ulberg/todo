Yes, buying licences in admin.microsoft.com needs Global Admin (or Billing Administrator), and your admin account no longer holds either standing. First check whether you need to buy anything at all, though: an **Entra ID P2 trial comes with 25 licences**, and you might have free ones already.

1. **Activate Global Admin** (asks for your passkey, max 1 h):
   https://portal.azure.com/#@adminazurestubble162simplel.onmicrosoft.com/view/Microsoft_Azure_PIMCommon/ActivationMenuBlade/~/aadmigratedroles
2. **Check for free licences.** Open the list below and see whether Microsoft Entra ID P2 shows more "Total" than "Assigned":
   https://portal.azure.com/#@adminazurestubble162simplel.onmicrosoft.com/view/Microsoft_AAD_IAM/LicensesMenuBlade/~/Products
   - **Licences free:** skip buying and go straight to assigning it to `endre-agent`.
   - **None free:** continue with step 3.
3. **Add one licence** to your existing P2 subscription. Signed in as the admin account, open Your products and click the Entra ID P2 entry → **Buy licences** (+1):
   https://admin.microsoft.com/#/subscriptions

   That's simpler than the catalog link I gave earlier, which buys a new product. It uses the payment method already on file for that subscription.

If admin.microsoft.com asks you to sign in, use the admin account (`endre@…onmicrosoft.com`), not your owner account. The owner now has billing in Azure only, no role in this tenant.
