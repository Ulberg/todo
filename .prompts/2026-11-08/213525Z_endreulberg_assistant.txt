Yes, that's almost certainly the problem. Nothing is lost; my checklist missed a step.

Azure has two separate permission systems:
- Entra roles, such as Global Administrator, control the directory: users, groups, app registrations.
- Azure roles, such as Owner, control subscriptions and the resources in them.

Being Global Admin gives no access to subscriptions. Only the owner account that created the subscription has Owner on it, so the admin sees 0.

Check it (2 minutes):

1. Sign in to portal.azure.com as the owner.
2. Open Subscriptions. The owner should see it, with "Default Directory" in the Directory column.
   - If the owner also sees 0, the signup really didn't finish. Go to Subscriptions → Add, or check that the card verification completed.
   - If it's there, carry on below.

Fix it (as the owner):

3. Open the subscription → Access control (IAM) → Add → Add role assignment.
4. Pick Owner, then the members "Endre Ulberg (Admin)", then Review + assign.
5. If the portal asks for conditions on the Owner role, choose the option that lets the person assign all roles. The bootstrap script needs to create role assignments.
6. Sign in as the admin. If the subscription still doesn't show, check the subscription filter: go to Settings (gear icon) → Directories + subscriptions, and make sure nothing is filtered out.

Why this is fine for now:
- The admin needs Owner to run the bootstrap. When Pulumi takes over, it turns that standing Owner assignment into a PIM-eligible role, so day to day you only hold Reader.
- The owner account keeps its Owner role as the billing and recovery fallback.

There's also a second route: as Global Admin, under Entra ID → Properties → "Access management for Azure resources", you can temporarily give yourself access to every subscription. You don't need it, since you have the owner account, and it's broader than necessary.
